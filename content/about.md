I am a developer living in Philadelphia, PA.
I have worked at a variety of startups in senior roles as both a full stack developer and a DevOps specialist,
including [VTS](https://www.vts.com/), [Relay Payments](https://www.relaypayments.com/), and [Logikcull](https://www.logikcull.com/) (acquired by Reveal AI).

My professional experience is mostly in Ruby and Ruby on Rails, but I have a broad array of experience in other technologies including Node/JS, TypeScript, Swift, Unity/C#, Unreal Engine, Houdini/VEX, and OpenGL/WebGL.
I have created a [ruby REPL](https://github.com/nassredean/fir), and a guide to [using Rails as an API](https://github.com/nassredean/rails5-api-guide).

I make [algorithmic art](https://www.instagram.com/nassredean.art) using techniques from computer graphics and computational geometry.

I currently work at [LeaseQuery](https://leasequery.com/) where I am helping build and scale their StackShine platform.
